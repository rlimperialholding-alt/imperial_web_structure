# Known failure patterns

Reject these patterns immediately.

## Weak or meaningless copy

- vague atmosphere without an offer;
- a feature promoted as the whole reason to buy;
- unexplained fragments such as standalone abbreviations;
- tautology such as "ready when it is ready to move into";
- internal system jargon such as quality gates in customer copy;
- robotic lists forced into a slogan;
- promises without a clear subject or causal link;
- CTAs such as "send the plot", "request a direction", "start the project" at the wrong funnel stage;
- condescending lines about the customer's intelligence;
- generic consultation copy duplicated across brands.

## Visual failures

- text, CTA or its background covers any protected part of the house or product;
- text remains inside its PowerPoint shape but overflows in the actual render;
- a word or logo glyph wraps to another line;
- the house is cropped without a deliberate art direction;
- the image becomes secondary to frames, disclaimers or panels;
- tiny legal copy appears on a feed creative;
- a gradient or panel system repeats across brands;
- contact-sheet inspection hides defects visible in the full render;
- pleasant image quality is mistaken for campaign quality.

## Gate failures

- the author approves their own work;
- PASS is written before final rendering;
- only file existence, HTTP status, font size or JSON validity is checked;
- a later version is accepted because it improved, not because it is flawless;
- reviewers see the intended diagnosis instead of independently evaluating the artifact;
- cross-brand review checks colors but not concept, copy sequence and geometry.
